Things have been weird in your recent work lately.  
Your co-workers were grumpy and more aggressive 
than before. In an attempt to maintain your peace
you decided to resign, and today was your last day 
as the company's employee.

It's 4:55:00PM. Five minutes left before you clock out. 
Collect your things, your bag, your favorite cookie, 
and the exit key. Survive, as your co-workers 
desperately try to catch you.

Controls: WASD/Arrow Keys.
Everything made by me. 
The audio was my first time actually trying to make music (and it sucks LOL, a first time for everything). 
I'm very glad with this project since I now polished the tile-map movement mechanic I've learned, as well as enemies. 
As a beginner game dev, this is a win for me LOLL.
